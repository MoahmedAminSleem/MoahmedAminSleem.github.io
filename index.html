THE ANALYST

ENTRY 01 · ٠١

I'm the analyst the factory calls when the numbers must be right.

At Marib International Garments, I design and ship the reporting ecosystem the whole factory runs on — performance, targets, incentives and executive reporting — built independently, end to end. Before that, I lived inside operations: customer data at Vodafone & Orange, logistics at SEDRA Group, personnel databases during military service. I learned how work actually flows long before I ever modeled it.

And no — I don't just "enter data." I architect where data is born, guard how it's cleaned, decide how it's stored, model how it moves, and translate what it means. The dashboard is the last 10% of the job; the other 90% is making the number deserve your trust.

I build AI-native — a vibe coder who directs AI like a workforce, then audits it like an accountant. The result: platforms that used to take teams, shipped by one person.
